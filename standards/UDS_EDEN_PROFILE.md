**Applicability:** All Eden Nodes, Seed Projects, Hardware/Firmware Builds, Governance Systems, and Commons Contributions

## Provenance & Scope

This document is an **Operation Eden profile of the Universal Diamond Standard**, not a verbatim reproduction of the source UDS documents.

The Drive corpus defines the Universal Diamond Standard through **eight core principles**:

1. Sovereignty
2. Transparency
3. Fairness
4. Accountability
5. Security
6. Service to Life
7. Privacy
8. Ecology

Operation Eden preserves those eight principles and adds two explicit physical-world profile extensions:

9. Subsidiarity & Decentralization
10. Preemptive Humility & Adaptive Regeneration

The Eden Profile therefore translates the UDS into a standard suitable for land, infrastructure, ecological restoration, distributed governance, open hardware, local data stewardship, and physical Node federation.

The source UDS Lite text remains under its original license. This Eden profile is a new operational adaptation for Operation Eden and does not purport to relicense the source document.

## 2. The Ten Eden Invariants

### I. Sovereignty
Systems must increase rather than diminish meaningful human agency. Participation must be voluntary, understandable, and reversible. No dark patterns, coercive manipulation, deceptive design, or captive-user mechanics.

### II. Transparency
Consequential processes, technical protocols, governance rules, resource allocations, limitations, and known failure modes must be documented in forms understandable to affected participants.

### III. Fairness
Systems must be evaluated for unequal access, discriminatory effects, exclusionary design, and capture by advantaged groups. Equal dignity does not require identical treatment, but arbitrary discrimination is prohibited.

### IV. Accountability
Every consequential system requires identifiable stewardship, correction pathways, incident reporting, and mechanisms for appeal or redress. Responsibility may be distributed; it may not disappear.

### V. Security
Systems must protect data integrity, operational continuity, authentication material, and physical infrastructure against foreseeable misuse, corruption, and failure. Security claims require evidence rather than assumption.

### VI. Service to Life
Technology, governance, and economics exist to support living beings and ecosystems. If an optimization degrades life merely to improve an abstract metric, the mechanism must be halted, examined, and redesigned.

### VII. Privacy, Consent & Data Sovereignty
Collect only what is necessary. Human and community data require meaningful consent, revocation, deletion or export pathways where applicable, and privacy-by-design architecture. Local-first and zero-write private modes are preferred where technically feasible.

### VIII. Ecology & Regeneration
Operation Eden extends the UDS ecological principle from environmental awareness to measurable regenerative practice. Physical interventions must establish baselines, document inputs, and evaluate whether soil, water, biodiversity, biomass, resilience, or other relevant ecological indicators improve over time.

### IX. Subsidiarity & Decentralization
Authority should reside at the most local practical level. Federation coordinates shared standards and interoperability; it does not become a command center for ordinary local life. No single node, interface, steward, or council receives unchecked system-wide control.

### X. Preemptive Humility & Adaptive Regeneration
No plan is treated as perfect. Assumptions remain challengeable. Failures, negative results, and ecological surprises are documented as shared intelligence. A design that cannot be corrected is not Eden-compliant.

## 3. Conformance Checklist

Before a repository, device, dataset, Seed Project, or physical Node is designated an official Operation Eden artifact:

- [ ] **Sovereignty:** Can participants decline, leave, or change participation without punitive friction?
- [ ] **Data:** Does the local community retain control of raw community and telemetry data?
- [ ] **Transparency:** Are decision rules, assumptions, dependencies, and known limitations documented?
- [ ] **Fairness:** Have unequal access, capture, exclusion, and distributional risks been evaluated?
- [ ] **Accountability:** Is there a named steward or responsible process and a correction path?
- [ ] **Security:** Are secrets, keys, access control, backups, and failure modes handled explicitly?
- [ ] **Offline Continuity:** Can essential local functions continue when external networks are unavailable?
- [ ] **Repairability:** Where hardware is involved, can common failures be diagnosed and repaired with reasonably accessible tools and parts?
- [ ] **Ecology:** Is there a measurable baseline and a defined method for evaluating environmental effects?
- [ ] **Commons:** Are reusable methods, results, and failure lessons published when privacy, safety, and consent permit?
- [ ] **License:** Does the artifact use the appropriate repository license and respect upstream license obligations?
- [ ] **Appeal:** Can affected participants challenge a decision or classification?

## 4. Open Infrastructure Rule

Operation Eden favors open, auditable, repairable infrastructure in foundational layers. Proprietary dependencies may be used when necessary only if their role, exit path, data implications, and replacement risk are documented.

A proprietary convenience must never become an invisible sovereignty dependency.

---

## 5. Physical-World Evidence Rule

For claims of regeneration, intention is insufficient.

A compliant project should preserve, where applicable:

1. pre-intervention baseline;
2. intervention protocol;
3. material and energy inputs;
4. telemetry or field measurements;
5. observation horizon;
6. measured outcomes;
7. uncertainty and confounders;
8. failures and corrective actions;
9. replication guidance.

> **The land is not required to agree with the plan. The plan is required to learn from the land.**

## 6. Relationship to the Parent UDS

The Eden Profile does not replace the broader Universal Diamond Standard. It applies that framework to:

- regenerative agriculture;
- ecological monitoring;
- open hardware;
- community governance;
- local data sovereignty;
- education;
- physical infrastructure;
- distributed Node federation.

Where this profile introduces an Eden-specific extension, that extension must remain labeled rather than retroactively attributed to the historical UDS source.

## 7. Revision Rule

Changes to this profile must preserve a public change record containing:

- revision identifier;
- proposed change;
- reason;
- source or evidence;
- affected systems;
- ratification status;
- migration requirements.

**Pattern must never overwrite record.**
