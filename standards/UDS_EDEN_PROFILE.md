# Universal Diamond Standard (UDS) — Eden Profile
**Document ID:** `UDS-EDEN-001`  
**Status:** Canonical Standard  
**Layer:** Layer 1 (Foundational Principles)  
**Applicability:** All Eden Nodes, Seed Projects, Hardware/Firmware Builds, and Commons Contributions

---

## 1. Mandate & Scope

The **Universal Diamond Standard (UDS) — Eden Profile** defines the non-negotiable ethical, operational, and architectural invariants for Operation Eden. Every project, device, dataset, and community node bearing the Eden designation must conform to these ten principles.

```text
                      [ SERVICE TO LIFE ]
                               ▲
          ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
          ▼                    ▼                    ▼
 [ SOVEREIGNTY ]      [ TRANSPARENCY ]      [ REGENERATION ]
 • Agency & Dignity   • Open Processes       • Net-Positive Impact
 • Consent & Privacy  • Explainable Tools     • Closed-Loop Cycles
```

---

## 2. The Ten Invariants

### I. Service to Life
Systems exist to serve living beings and natural ecosystems. Human beings and living ecology do not exist to serve systems. If any technological, governance, or economic mechanism degrades life to optimize an abstract metric, it must be halted and redesigned.

### II. Inherent Sovereignty & Non-Extraction
Participants are sovereign contributors, not products, behavioral assets, demographic extraction targets, or captive users. Systems must never rely on psychological manipulation, dark patterns, or structural coercion.

### III. Radical Transparency & Explainability
Decision-making pipelines, governance mechanics, technical protocols, and resource allocations must be auditable and visible to those affected by them. Closed proprietary dependencies are prohibited in foundational layers.

### IV. Informed & Revocable Consent
All participation, data collection, telemetry streaming, and economic interactions require explicit, informed, and revocable consent. Opt-out must be painless, zero-write private modes must be supported, and exit must be friction-free.

### V. Measurable Ecological Regeneration
Every physical node or agricultural intervention must produce demonstrable, verifiable ecological improvement over time. Interventions must aim for net gains in topsoil health, biodiversity, watershed clarity, and biomass.

### VI. Subsidiarity & Decentralization
Authority and decision-making power must reside at the most local practical level. No single node, council, or interface may establish centralized gatekeeping or unilateral veto over autonomous nodes.

### VII. Radical Openness & Commons Contribution
Methods, designs, failure logs, and technical specifications must be contributed to the public Knowledge Commons under reciprocal open licenses (CERN-OHL-S, Apache-2.0, CC BY-SA 4.0). A solution hidden is a solution diminished.

### VIII. Graceful Failure & Offline Continuity
Digital and physical infrastructure must operate autonomously during network disruptions, power outages, and external collapse. Systems must fail gracefully without taking local life-support or sovereign data down with them.

### IX. Due Process & Non-Punitive Resolution
Dispute resolution, steward removal, and protocol challenges must adhere to documented due process, contextual evaluation, and restorative mediation rather than punitive exclusion or algorithmic social credit.

### X. Preemptive Humility & Dynamic Adaptation
No plan is utopia. Assumptions are subject to real-world feedback, ecological shifts, and community critique. Failure is documented openly as shared intelligence.

## 3. Conformance Checklist for Eden Projects

Before a repository, hardware revision, or local project is designated an official Eden artifact, it must satisfy:

- [ ] **Data Check:** Does the user/community retain raw data ownership?
- [ ] **Dependency Check:** Can this system function if the external network is severed?
- [ ] **License Check:** Are all source files under CERN-OHL, Apache-2.0, or CC-BY-SA?
- [ ] **Repairability Check:** Can the hardware be repaired with standard hand tools and off-the-shelf components?
- [ ] **Ecological Check:** Does the lifecycle of this artifact minimize toxic waste and environmental harm?
