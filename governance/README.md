# Operation Eden Governance

This directory contains the constitutional and operational governance layer for Operation Eden.

## Governing Principle

**Decision power should exist as close as practical to the people affected by the decision.**

Operation Eden is designed as a federation of locally autonomous Nodes and working groups rather than a centralized command hierarchy.

## Planned Artifacts

- Minimum Viable Governance Charter
- proposal lifecycle specification
- stewardship scopes and term limits
- transparency requirements
- conflict mediation and restorative processes
- appeal and correction procedures
- amendment process
- financial accountability rules
- Node federation / interoperability agreements

## Design Requirements

Governance mechanisms should preserve:

- consent
- sovereignty
- transparency
- accountability
- dignity
- meaningful exit
- local autonomy
- anti-capture safeguards

No governance mechanism is considered successful merely because it produces decisions. It must also preserve the agency and dignity of the people governed by it.
